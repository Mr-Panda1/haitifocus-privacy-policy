Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 30, 2025

HaitiFocus does not collect, store, or process any personal user data.

Data Collection
This app does not require user accounts, authentication, or registration.
We do not collect names, email addresses, location data, device identifiers, cookies, or tracking information.

App Functionality
HaitiFocus only fetches and displays publicly available content from third-party news websites and RSS feeds. Users read content anonymously within the app.

Third-Party Content
Any data collection, cookies, or tracking that may occur on third-party websites is governed by those websites’ own privacy policies. HaitiFocus does not control, access, or store any such data.

Data Sharing
Since HaitiFocus does not collect personal data, no user data is shared with third parties.

Children’s Privacy
HaitiFocus is not directed at children under the age of 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security
Because no personal data is collected or stored by the app, there is no personal user data at risk.
